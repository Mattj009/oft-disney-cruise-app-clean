The Palo Ad Showdown

Same assignment. Same brunch. Same basic joke. Two different AIs went into the galley, and two very different ads came out.

← Back to Bonus Vault
Exhibit A

The Grok Version

This one is fascinating. For whatever reason, Grok got unusually cautious and started acting like Disney’s lawyers were hiding under the buffet table.

  • Timid branding.
  • Awkward use of space.
  • A less focused layout.
  • And, most tragically, a generic waffle where a Mickey waffle clearly wanted to be.

It gets the point across. But only after nervously looking over its shoulder.

Exhibit B

The ChatGPT Version

This version understood the assignment. It leaned into the actual joke: Palo brunch is so good, it’s worth wearing pants for.

  • Better use of the page.
  • More visual presence for the food.
  • A stronger punchline.
  • A look that fits the overall cruise-app style far better.

In other words: less apologizing, more brunch.

🏆Final Judgment

The Grok version survives as an important historical document: proof that even a usually fearless AI can suddenly become weirdly shy about trademarks.

Winner: ChatGPT — by a waffle.

Either way, the real winner is Palo. And yes — it’s still worth wearing pants for.
